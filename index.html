OpenQuantum

From now on the quantum world is no longer mysterious to ordinary people — it becomes an everyday topic, even for kids.

Status

Founding — initialized, first release in preparation

What it is

The school’s quantum hall, built to the OpenMath standard — real interactive things first, never hand-waved mysticism. The core wonders, made touchable: superposition, the observer/measurement problem, entanglement, uncertainty, wave–particle duality, and the double-slit experiment as the founding exhibit. Every analogy is labelled as a finger, not the moon — the hall teaches where each picture breaks. Every card separates what experiments show, what interpretations argue, and what sellers invent.

END GOAL

OpenQuantum fills the gap its founder named plainly: quantum mechanics is his favourite topic, and he does not fully understand it. DawnSci computes the numbers; OpenQuantum makes a ten-year-old able to ask the right questions about them. Interpretations — Copenhagen, many-worlds, pilot-wave — are labelled as interpretations, under the school’s truth-label discipline.

ROADMAP

  • Double-slit, playable: fire one particle at a time, watch the pattern assemble — the single most honest magic trick in physics.
  • The moon question, as a game: what changes when you look — measurement as interaction, not eyesight.
  • 指月之指 itself, as the hall’s entrance text: words about quantum are fingers; the experiments are where you look up.
